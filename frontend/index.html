GOVERNMENT GUIDELINES

MoRTH – Road Accident Assistance Guidelines

Guidelines for Road Accident Response

In the event of a road accident, the primary objective shall be to protect life, prevent further injury, and ensure timely medical assistance.

  • 1
    Ensure Safety. Immediately assess the accident scene. Do not put yourself or others at additional risk. Where possible, alert approaching traffic and maintain a safe distance from damaged vehicles, fire, fuel leakage, or other hazards.
  • 2
    Report the Accident. Any person witnessing or discovering a road accident may immediately dial 112 and provide the accident location, nature of the incident, approximate number of victims, and requirement for an ambulance. The 112 responder may coordinate ambulance services and provide information regarding the nearest designated hospital.
  • 3
    Provide Immediate Assistance. A Good Samaritan may either request an ambulance through 112 or, where appropriate, arrange transportation of the injured person to the nearest designated hospital. Assistance should be provided in good faith and without unnecessarily delaying medical treatment.
  • 4
    Hospital Admission and Treatment. The injured person should be taken to a designated hospital at the earliest possible opportunity. Emergency medical treatment should not be delayed because of formalities or financial concerns.
  • 5
    Cooperation with Authorities. Provide accurate information about the accident when requested. However, a Good Samaritan who reports an accident or transports a victim is entitled to legal protections and should not be subjected to unnecessary harassment or delay.
  • 6
    Golden Hour. Immediate assistance is critical during the Golden Hour, the period following a traumatic injury when prompt medical care can significantly improve the likelihood of preventing death.
    • 🏥

    Remember: Report. Rescue. Reach the Hospital. Save a Life.

    LEGAL INFORMATION

    Good Samaritan Guidelines

    What to Do at a Road Accident

    1. 1
      Ensure your own safety first. Stop at a safe location and avoid creating additional danger to yourself, the victim, or other road users.
    2. 2
      Call emergency services. Dial 112 to report the road accident. Provide the accident location, approximate number of victims, their apparent condition, and request an ambulance. The 112 system can coordinate with ambulance services and provide information about the nearest designated hospital.
    3. 3
      Provide reasonable assistance. If it is safe and necessary, assist the injured person and arrange transportation to the nearest appropriate hospital. Avoid actions that may unnecessarily worsen serious injuries.
    4. 4
      Do not delay medical treatment. The priority should be to ensure that the injured person receives emergency medical attention as quickly as possible.

    Rights and Protection of a Good Samaritan

    Under the Motor Vehicles Act and Good Samaritan Rules, a person who informs the police about an accident or transports an accident victim to a hospital should be treated respectfully and normally be permitted to leave without being subjected to unnecessary requirements.

    Providing one's name, contact details and other personal information is generally voluntary. A Good Samaritan who only calls emergency services cannot be compelled to disclose personal details. Good Samaritans are also protected against unnecessary civil or criminal liability arising from their genuine assistance.

    ⚠️

    Remember: In a road accident, timely assistance during the critical period after injury can help save a life. Call 112, seek medical assistance, and help responsibly.
